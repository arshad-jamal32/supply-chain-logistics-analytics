# Phase 1: Overall Business Performance & KPI Analysis

## Key Performance Indicators

| KPI | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Revenue | 36,784,734.31 |
| Total Profit | 3,966,902.97 |
| Total Orders | 65,752 |
| Total Order Items | 180,519 |
| Total Quantity Sold | 384,079 |
| Average Order Value | 559.45 |
| Average Profit per Order | 60.33 |
| Overall Profit Margin | 10.78% |

## Business Insights

- The business generated total revenue of 36.78 million.
- Total recorded profit was approximately 3.97 million.
- The dataset contains 65,752 unique orders and 180,519 order items.
- Average Order Value was 559.45.
- Overall profit margin was 10.78%.

## Methodology

- Order-level metrics were calculated after grouping records by Order Id.
- Total order items were calculated using individual dataset records.
- Average Order Value = Total Revenue / Total Orders.
- Average Profit per Order = Total Profit / Total Orders.
- Profit Margin = (Total Profit / Total Revenue) × 100.

## Note

These are overall recorded dataset metrics. Order status and cancellation impacts will be investigated separately in subsequent analysis phases.
